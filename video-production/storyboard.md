# Storyboard and timeline

**This video tells** portfolio climate-risk analysts and technical reviewers **that** climate risk can reach a portfolio through suppliers, power and ports it doesn't own, and that the FIN-04 Climate Exposure Graph searches for exactly those hidden exposures — honestly labelled, on synthetic data.

Format: 1920×1080, 30 fps, 4:53 (293.05 s). Times below are from `assets/timeline.json` (built from the measured voice durations). Each scene is one HyperFrames sub-composition in `compositions/`; scenes overlap by 0.5 s to cross-fade. The full spoken text with exact line times is in `narration.md`.

Visual identity: "midnight cartography" — ink-dark canvas (#0c1016), the app's own alabaster pages as lit documents, gold (#d4ae3c) for insight, vermilion (#e0553b) for a climate shock, green (#62b88d) only for "clear". Type: Instrument Serif (headlines), Archivo Black (hook kinetic words), Space Grotesk (labels), Inter (body/footnotes), JetBrains Mono (numbers and formulas) — all from the starter kit (SIL OFL).

## Audio plan
- **Voice**: Kokoro-82M `af_heart` (Apache-2.0 weights), local, 46 lines placed at measured times.
- **Music**: original score generated by `scripts/make_music.py` (additive synthesis + convolution reverb, seeded), arranged to the scene boundaries: tension (hook) → minor (problem) → lift (idea) → steady pulse (demo) → brighter (engine) → warm build (impact) → resolution to D major (title).
- **Ducking**: the bed sits about 17 dB under the voice while it speaks and opens to about 9 dB under between lines; fades 90 ms / 550 ms.
- **SFX**: 43 cues from the starter kit's code-made sounds, 15–22 dB under the voice; no whooshes, no sounds on cards entering or leaving.
- **Master**: −16 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1.5 dBTP.
